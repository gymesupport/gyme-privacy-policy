Privacy Policy for Gyme

Effective Date: Sunday, May 3rd 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Gyme. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at gymesupport@gmail.com.

1. What information do we collect?

Local Data Storage
Gyme is designed primarily as a local-first application. Your core user-generated content—including your workout schedules, exercise names, routines, workout history, and any images you select from your Photo Library—is stored on your local device.

In-App Purchases
If you choose to purchase a Gyme Premium subscription or lifetime unlock, the transaction is processed securely by Apple via the App Store. Your payment information is handled directly by Apple, and we receive verification receipts to unlock the premium features within the app.

Customer Support
If you voluntarily contact us via email for support or feedback, we will use your email address and the contents of your message to respond to your specific inquiry.

2. How do we use your information?

Your workout data is used locally on your device to provide the core functionality of the Gyme application. We are committed to not selling your personal data to third parties.

3. Will your information be shared with anyone?

We prioritize your privacy and will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Relevant data exchanges occur securely between your device and Apple (for the purpose of verifying App Store purchases and processing In-App Payments). Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy for more information on how they handle your App Store data.

4. How long do we keep your information?

Your data is kept indefinitely on your personal device until you choose to delete it. Because the data is stored locally in the app's document directory, completely uninstalling the Gyme app from your device will permanently delete all of your saved workouts, schedules, and images stored within the app.

5. Do we make updates to this notice?

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. We may notify you of material changes by posting a notice within the app or through App Store release notes.

6. How can you contact us about this notice?

If you have questions or comments about this notice or the Gyme app, you may email us at:
gymesupport@gmail.com
