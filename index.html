Privacy Policy for Gyme

Effective Date: Sunday, May 3rd 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Gyme. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at gymesupport@gmail.com.

1. What information do we collect?

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers.
Gyme is designed to be a local-first application. All of your user-generated content—including your workout schedules, exercise names, routines, workout history, and any images you select from your Photo Library—is stored exclusively on your local device. We have no access to this information.

In-App Purchases
If you choose to purchase a Gyme Premium subscription or lifetime unlock, the transaction is processed entirely and securely by Apple via the App Store. We do not collect, process, or have access to your payment information (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses). We only receive anonymous verification receipts from Apple to unlock the premium features within the app.

2. How do we use your information?

Because we do not collect your personal data, we do not use, sell, or share it with any third parties. Your workout data is yours alone and is only used locally on your device to provide the core functionality of the Gyme application.

3. Will your information be shared with anyone?

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties. The only external data exchange occurs securely between your device and Apple (for the purpose of verifying App Store purchases and processing In-App Payments). Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy for more information on how they handle your App Store data.

4. How long do we keep your information?

Your data is kept indefinitely on your personal device until you choose to delete it. Because the data is stored locally in the app's document directory, completely uninstalling the Gyme app from your device will permanently delete all of your saved workouts, schedules, and images.

5. Do we make updates to this notice?

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. If we make material changes to this privacy notice, we may notify you either by prominently posting a notice of such changes or by directly sending you a notification.

6. How can you contact us about this notice?

If you have questions or comments about this notice or the Gyme app, you may email us at:
gymesupport@gmail.com
